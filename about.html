About EAK International School

Founded under the auspices of EAK Pustak Pvt. Ltd., EAK International School has championed child-centric pedagogical innovation, scientific inquiry, and global ethics for over a decade.

FOUNDATIONAL ETHOS

Vision, Mission & Core Values

Our Vision

To be an internationally distinguished beacon of learning that nurtures intellectually fearless, emotionally resilient, and socially compassionate global leaders.

Our Mission

To deliver a balanced, rigorous curriculum supported by world-class infrastructure, empowering each learner to discover their unique genius through critical thinking.

Our Core Ethos

Uncompromising integrity, mutual respect, environmental stewardship, empathy, and lifelong curiosity guiding every classroom interaction.

Dr. Alistair Vance

Ph.D. Education (Oxon), M.Ed. (Harvard)

Principal & Head of School
LEADERSHIP PERSPECTIVE

"Education is the architecture of the human spirit."

At EAK International School, we reject passive memorization. We believe children flourish when challenged with authentic questions, provided with modern tools, and encouraged to embrace calculated intellectual risks.

Whether writing code in modern computer labs, participating in inter-house debates, or training in our swimming pool and sports grounds, every EAK student is nurtured for academic excellence, character, and lifelong success.

OUR HERITAGE

A Decade of Educational Pioneering

From our founding vision under EAK Pustak Pvt. Ltd. to an internationally benchmarked K–12 institution.

2012

Foundation by EAK Pustak

Established with 250 founding students, pioneering progressive pedagogy that replaced passive textbook recitation with active hands-on exploration.

2016

Cambridge International Accreditation

Accredited by Cambridge Assessment International Education alongside national CBSE affiliation, creating our flagship dual-pathway continuum.

2020

Inauguration of Turing STEM & AI Hubs

Expanded campus infrastructure with advanced robotics, 3D prototyping suites, and computer science workstations for Grades 6 through 12.

2026

Olympic Sports Complex & Green Canopy

Commissioned our semi-Olympic heated pool, sunken amphitheatre, and 100% solar rooftop array serving over 2,400 enrolled learners.
