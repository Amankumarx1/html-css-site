COMMON QUERIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions regarding admissions, curriculum choices, and student life.

What curriculum options are offered at EAK International School?
EAK International School provides a dual integrated continuum combining CBSE and Cambridge Assessment International Education. In Primary and Middle school, students receive experiential inquiry-led learning, while in Grades 9–12 they can choose between CBSE Board Examinations and Cambridge IGCSE / A-Levels.
What is the teacher-to-student ratio in classrooms?
We maintain an average ratio of 1:12 across all grades. Class cohorts are strictly capped at 24 students per section to ensure individual attention, regular diagnostic feedback, and strong teacher-parent relationships.
Does the school provide bus transportation and GPS tracking?
Yes, our air-conditioned bus fleet covers five major corridors across Delhi NCR. 100% of buses feature speed governors, CCTV cameras, female attendants, and real-time RFID tap cards so parents can track student entry and exit.
How are mid-term transfers and relocations accommodated?
Mid-term admissions are considered subject to seat vacancies in the respective grade. Parents should submit the student's previous school report cards and a valid Transfer Certificate (TC) signed by the competent educational authority.
Are merit or sibling scholarships available?
Yes, EAK offers up to 25% merit scholarships for students demonstrating exceptional academic aptitude in diagnostic assessments or state/national level sports distinction. A 10% sibling concession is also applicable on base tuition for the younger sibling.
What sports and co-curricular facilities are available on campus?
Students have daily access to an all-weather 50m heated semi-Olympic swimming pool, FIFA-standard football turf, basketball and badminton courts, the Turing Robotics & AI Lab, and our 1,200-seat amphitheatre.
